Where Your Donations Go?

At Prandhara Green Hope Foundation, integrity and accountability are our foundation blocks. We are proud to share a granular breakdown of how individual, corporate, and public donations are allocated to our active green and social initiatives.

Guaranteed Accountability

We employ strict auditing practices to ensure that 90% of all public contributions directly fund grassroots environmental projects in rural schools and municipal lands.

Our tree plantation programs utilize native species (like Neem, Peepal, Banyan, and Karanj) which have high survival rates and rich ecological benefits, ensuring that every contribution leaves a long-term carbon footprint reduction.

📌 Tax Exemption Benefit: All donations to Prandhara Green Hope Foundation are eligible for tax deductions under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act, India.

🌳 Tree Plantation & Reforestation Drives 40%

Covers native sapling sourcing, soil preparation, trenching, tree guards, and maintenance for up to 3 years.

☀️ Rural Solar Energy Electrification Drives 30%

Funds solar roof installations in rural schools, solar micro-grids, and off-grid irrigation water pumps.

🌿 Eco Awareness, Workshops & Eco-Clubs 20%

Supports environmental education materials, plastic cleanliness drives, and Eco-Clubs in school campuses.

⚙️ Essential Administration & Field Equipment 10%

Covers basic transport, tools (spades, water pipes), volunteer field safety gear, and auditing fees.
