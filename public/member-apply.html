Why Join As a Member?

Members form the visionary core of Prandhara Green Hope Foundation. As an official registered member, you are directly involved in organizing tree plantation drives, leading eco-clubs, and coordinating environmental awareness events across Maharashtra.

Exclusive Privileges for Prandhara Members:

  • Receive a unique, verifiable digital **Official Identity Card**.
  • Right to vote in our annual environmental planning committee.
  • Certificate of Active Service for leading local tree plantation blocks.
  • Access to our exclusive seed vaults and planting nurseries.
  • Invitations to premium state-level sustainability forums and seminars.

🌿 Membership is completely free for all environment-loving citizens above 18. We expect members to attend at least three field events annually.
