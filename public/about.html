Prandhara Green Hope Foundation

Founded with a passion for nature, we are a committed environmental non-governmental organization (NGO) based in Aurangabad, Maharashtra.

Our primary focus is environmental conservation, ecological restoration, sustainable development, and rural empowerment. Prandhara represents a voice of conviction in sustainability, driving active change through community-led tree plantation drives, water body conservation, and clean solar energy initiatives.

We work to build a greener tomorrow where scientific research, traditional wisdom, community participation, and active policy advocacy converge to secure a sustainable ecosystem.

In this rapidly changing world, climate change and habitat loss present new challenges every day. We believe that conservation is a continuous journey that requires social, economic, and scientific adaptability.

We firmly believe that without active community participation and empowerment, no ecosystem or natural resource can be sustained. Prandhara is governed by a dynamic group of professionals, supported technically by environmental specialists, social workers, and dedicated volunteers.

Our Vision

To build a future where humans and nature coexist harmoniously, with empowered communities actively protecting ecosystems, biodiversity, and natural resources for generations to come.

Our Mission

To conserve wildlife, ecology, natural resources, and sustainable livelihoods through scientific research, community participation, environmental education, and policy advocacy for a greener and sustainable future.
